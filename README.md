# CAD-MODEL-GSLV-MK3
3D CAD assembly of ISRO’s GSLV Mk III (LVM3), showcasing its twin S200 solid boosters, L110 liquid core stage, and C25 cryogenic upper stage. Developed using publicly available references to explore aerospace design, multistage rocket architecture, and component integration.

# GSLV Mk III (LVM3) Rocket Assembly | CAD Modelling & Assembly Design

## Project Overview

This project presents a 3D CAD assembly of the **Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle Mark III (GSLV Mk III)**, officially designated **Launch Vehicle Mark-3 (LVM3)**, developed by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

The assembly represents the launch vehicle's major structural and functional components, illustrating its three-stage configuration, twin solid-propellant strap-on boosters, liquid-propellant core stage, and cryogenic upper stage.

The model is developed with reference to publicly available technical information and configuration details published by ISRO. It aims to provide an engineering-oriented visual representation of India's heavy-lift launch vehicle.

**Official ISRO Reference:** [Launch Vehicle Mark-3 (LVM3) — ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/Launchers.html)

## Vehicle Configuration

The LVM3 is a three-stage launch vehicle designed to place heavy communication satellites into geosynchronous transfer orbit and support missions to other orbits. Its propulsion architecture combines solid, liquid, and cryogenic propulsion systems.

The assembly represents the following major components:

- **Payload Fairing:** The protective nose enclosure that accommodates the payload and shields it from aerodynamic and thermal environments during atmospheric ascent.
- **C25 Cryogenic Upper Stage:** The upper stage powered by the CE-20 cryogenic engine, using liquid oxygen and liquid hydrogen propellants.
- **L110 Liquid Core Stage:** The liquid-propellant core stage powered by two Vikas engines, providing propulsion during the vehicle's ascent.
- **Two S200 Solid Strap-On Boosters:** Large solid-propellant boosters positioned on opposite sides of the core vehicle to provide substantial thrust during the initial phase of flight.
- **Interstage and Structural Sections:** Structural interfaces connecting the propulsion stages and supporting the overall vehicle configuration.
- **Payload and Upper-Stage Interfaces:** Components representing the payload accommodation and integration arrangements.

> **Note:** The exact components represented in this CAD assembly depend on the individual parts modelled and included in this repository.

## Engineering Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to understand and represent the configuration of a heavy-lift launch vehicle through 3D CAD modelling and assembly integration.

Key objectives include:

- Reconstructing the overall geometry and arrangement of the LVM3 using publicly available reference material.
- Understanding the structural arrangement of a launch vehicle combining solid, liquid, and cryogenic propulsion.
- Representing the integration of twin S200 strap-on boosters with the central vehicle structure.
- Organising individual CAD parts into a coherent full-vehicle assembly.
- Studying the relationship between stage arrangement, propulsion architecture, and overall launch vehicle configuration.
- Developing practical skills in aerospace CAD modelling, component integration, and technical visualisation.

## Technical Background

The LVM3 employs a combination of three major propulsion elements:

1. **S200 Solid Boosters:** Provide high initial thrust during launch.
2. **L110 Liquid Core Stage:** Uses two Vikas engines to provide sustained propulsion during ascent.
3. **C25 Cryogenic Upper Stage:** Uses the CE-20 engine to provide upper-stage propulsion for the intended orbital insertion.

This configuration enables LVM3 to support heavy-payload missions, including geosynchronous satellite launches and human-spaceflight-related missions.
